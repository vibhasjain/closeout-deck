## Design Context

### Users
Healthcare staffing platform leaders, agency operations teams, facility staffing teams, and clinicians evaluating how Closeout Agent reconciles completed shifts. The page must quickly explain how evidence becomes an auditable decision so each party can trust pay, billing, and exception handling.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, and human. The experience should feel operationally precise without becoming cold, technical, or cartoonish. It should create confidence that Closeout Agent can coordinate delicate work across clinicians, staffing agencies, and facilities.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, editorial enterprise presentation with clinical warmth: photoreal people at natural proportions, restrained HyperTrack green and teal accents, deep navy typography, generous space, and clear evidence-led diagrams. Avoid cartoon figures, generic AI gloss, decorative gradients, abrupt image crops, and visual effects that weaken trust.

### Design Principles
1. Tell the human story before exposing system mechanics.
2. Keep every visual enterprise-credible, photoreal, and emotionally grounded.
3. Adapt compositions intentionally across screen sizes; preserve people and dissolve unavoidable image boundaries instead of showing hard seams.
4. Serve imagery at an appropriate pixel density and compression level so source detail remains sharp without wasting bandwidth.
5. Preserve navigation, accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, and all existing deck functionality during visual changes.
