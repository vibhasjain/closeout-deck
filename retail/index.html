HyperTrack 01 / 07

01 · Why we're here

We're here to lower what you pay for contingent labor.

You don't overpay because rates are high. You overpay because at peak, nobody has time to check. Every hour, across every tier, billed through an agency. Margins that won't stretch. Churn that eats reliability. Compliance that changes by county.

02 · The moment of truth

The shift doesn't end at clock-out. It ends when she's paid.

She chose this agency over two others, and she'll choose again when next week's shifts post. Time-to-pay is the single biggest reason talent picks one agency over another. Which makes it your problem too.

03 · Bill & pay

Their middle office, your accounts payable — the promise breaks in between.

Their ops validate punches by hand. Your AP approves invoices it can't check. Neither scales. HyperTrack already validates time for more than a million shifts a month — and can now validate agency invoices.

04 · Your side of the ledger

Now put your invoice in the middle.

Agency invoice, your facility's timesheets, location trail, approved schedule. Laid on one timeline for the same shift. Where they agree, it pays. Where they don't, your AP has the evidence before the money leaves the building.

05 · Closed out, automatically

Then it closes out in your VMS. Agentically.

The same digital worker reads the agency invoice, reconciles it against the shifts that were actually worked, and posts the verified result into the VMS you already run. Clean invoices pass straight through. Disputed lines wait, with their evidence attached.

06 · Why it comes back to you

Pay him right and he comes back to your building.

Every disputed timesheet, every meal break logged wrong, every missing clock-out erodes the trust that brought him here. Close it right in this pay cycle and he requests your site next week. Let it sit for two pay cycles and he finds another job.

07 · One coin

Accurate billing and reliable service are two sides of the same coin.

Pay your agencies precisely and quickly, and the good ones send you their best people first. Let invoices sit in dispute, and you get whoever is left. Your accounts payable team is a talent strategy.
