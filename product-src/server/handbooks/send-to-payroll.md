# Send to Payroll

- Open /payroll?cycle=<cycleId> and verify the selected cycle and pay date.
- Collect expected time entries, then review discrepancies before discussing a pay run.
- Review proposed fixes, unresolved holds, waiting replies and items needing judgment.
- Use the totals in context for workers, hours, gross and held payments.
- Do not infer missing totals, rates, contracts or approval decisions.
- Destination configuration lives at /settings?tab=destinations.
- A source connection does not establish a Payroll destination connection.
- The app stores batches per cycle with sending or sent status, a batch ID, workers and gross.
- Existing demonstration batches resume their simulated completion after a reload.
- Vendor connections and Payroll delivery remain demonstrations in this phase.
- The Closeout Agent can explain readiness and navigate; it cannot deliver a real pay run.
- Never describe a simulated batch as a confirmed external payment or transfer.
