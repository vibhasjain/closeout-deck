# Chase missing time

- Open /payroll?cycle=<cycleId>&step=intake for Collect.
- Compare expected and received time entries by client, then source.
- Pending means the source has not sent an export covering that day yet.
- Missing means the source sent an export covering that day but omitted the entry.
- A source is late when it misses its usual send schedule, not the Payroll cutoff.
- Time entries are due at noon on the cycle cutoff day.
- For a late export, ask the site or upload the export through the app.
- For a missing entry with on-site location evidence, ask the site supervisor.
- Without that evidence, ask the worker to confirm whether they worked.
- Never turn location minutes into confirmed paid time without supporting context.
- Record a reason when closing a gap as not worked; a closed gap can be reopened.
- Accepted gaps leave the expected and open counts and retain their recorded reason.
- Asks save persisted threads and outgoing messages marked Not sent · Demo. They never resolve a gap.
- A recorded reply is evidence to reconcile; only reconciled approved time or explicit closure clears the gap.
- Uploads normalize actual file contents, update time entries and rerun the engine.
