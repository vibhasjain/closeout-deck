# Mediation

- Open /payroll/<full shiftId>?cycle=<cycleId> for a time entry and its conversation.
- Worker and facility conversations are separate for each entry and cycle.
- Prioritize holds before flags; contract and policy stops precede related calculations.
- Ask the worker about actual arrival, departure, overlapping hours or meal times.
- Ask the facility about contracts, rates, schedule changes, deductions or edited records.
- A missing signed meal waiver is a facility question; meal timing is a worker question.
- Use only recorded clock or schedule times; never invent evidence or replies.
- Prepare a concise question about the unresolved fact and explain why it matters.
- Drafts can be edited, dismissed or sent through the app's conversation controls.
- An unsent composer is saved separately from the audit trail.
- Only an explicit send action records an asked-at timestamp and a send trail entry.
- Seeded sample conversations are examples, not real delivery events.
- Current messaging is a browser demonstration; do not claim external delivery.
