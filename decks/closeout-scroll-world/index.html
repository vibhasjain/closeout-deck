Story experiment

01 The worker

The work is done.
Pay shouldn’t be a question.

A missing clock-out, disputed break, or late confirmation can hold up money someone already earned. The worker should not have to chase the shift after living it.

  • Finished shift
  • Fair pay
  • No chasing

02 The staffing agency

Every shift holds
two promises.

Pay the worker correctly. Bill the customer accurately. When schedule, clock, location, and human evidence disagree, both promises—and the agency’s trust—are exposed.

  • Payroll
  • Billing
  • Margin & trust

03 The operations team

Your best people shouldn’t
spend the night chasing clocks.

Ops becomes the manual glue between workers, facilities, systems, and policy—reviewing every mismatch because nobody else owns the close.

  • Exception queues
  • Worker outreach
  • Facility follow-up

04 The Closeout Agent

One agent orchestrates
the entire close.

It gathers missing facts, talks to the right people, applies the customer’s rulebook, resolves what it can, and shows its work when a human decision is required.

  • Collect
  • Reconcile
  • Resolve

05 The settled shift

One shift.
Everyone can move.

The worker is paid correctly. The agency can bill with confidence. Ops reviews only the genuine exception. Every decision carries its evidence and reasoning.

  • Paid correctly
  • Approval-ready
  • Auditable
