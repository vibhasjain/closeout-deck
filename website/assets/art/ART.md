# Website art — Green Risograph Monumentalism

Seven images, one language. Every prompt is the STYLE block verbatim, a blank line, then the SCENE paragraph verbatim. These are the emotional beats of an enterprise homepage; the product proof is real UI elsewhere on the page, so the art never shows screens.

## STYLE (prefix for every image — do not vary)

A risograph print, landscape 16:9. Two spot inks only, printed on warm cream paper (#efe9dc): a deep charcoal-green ink (#0f1f17) for shadows and solid masses, and HyperTrack green (#22c55e) for mid-tones and lit surfaces, with the bare cream paper serving as the highlight and a hot pale-cream glow at the brightest edges where light rakes across stone, glass or metal. Heavy stochastic grain and halftone stipple across every surface, visible ink texture, slight misregistration of the two inks at hard edges, and soft fog gradients that dissolve the far side of the scene into blank paper. Monumentalism: the architecture of shift work rendered at colossal, temple-like scale, strong one-point or low three-quarter perspective, a wide empty foreground, and tiny human figures for scale. Quiet, serious, cinematic, dawn or dusk light. No text, letters, numbers, logos, signage, flags, watermarks, screens or UI anywhere. No cartoon, no clay, no photoreal, no glossy 3D render, no third colour.

## Scenes

### 01 · hero — "The fastest growing staffing companies run on HyperTrack."
Dawn. A colossal distribution center rises out of low fog like a temple: a long, flat-roofed facade of loading doors and columns, ten storeys tall, lit from the right by a low sun that turns the top edge of the building to hot cream. Wide shallow steps run the full width of the foreground. A stream of tiny shift workers, dozens of them, walks up the steps toward the open central bay, some with duffel bags, a few in hi-vis vests. Along the ground, one thin line of green light runs from the bottom of the frame up the steps and into the entrance, the only saturated green in the picture. The far ends of the building fade into paper. Camera at ground level, slightly off-centre, looking up.

### 02 · two-monuments — "Clients and workers both choose again every day."
Dusk on a wide, empty plain. Two monumental buildings stand a little apart, each the size of a stadium, drawn in the same architecture: stacked floors, long horizontal bands of windows, a broad entrance at ground level. The left one is dark and silent, its windows unlit, fog eating its upper floors. The right one is alive: every window glowing green, a soft green halo on the ground around it. Between them, on the plain, hundreds of tiny figures walk from the dark building toward the lit one, forming a thin river of people. Only the lit building's windows and halo are green. Low camera, long shadows, grain heavy in the sky.

### 03 · middle-office — "AI already runs recruiting. The middle office is next."
Interior of a back office built at the scale of a cathedral: a high vaulted hall with endless rows of identical desks receding into fog, each desk stacked with paper, filing cabinets lining the walls like pews, tall windows admitting pale dawn light in long diagonal shafts. In the near foreground one tiny clerk sits at one desk under one lamp. From the far end of the hall a single thread of green light enters through the distant doorway and runs along the central aisle toward the clerk, not yet reaching the desk. Only the thread of light is green. One-point perspective down the aisle, camera at desk height.

### 04 · one-line — "One line from the clipboard to the paycheck."
A monumental conveyor crosses an open landscape like a Roman aqueduct, left to right across the whole frame: a raised stone-and-steel belt carried on seven great pylons, each pylon a station with a small lit chamber at its top. At the far left the line begins at a small, plain building shaped like a clipboard standing on its end; at the far right it ends at a broad, columned building like a bank or treasury. Tiny figures work at the base of each pylon. Fog hides the ground between them. The seven station chambers glow green, the only green in the image; the belt itself is dark ink. Low camera, dawn light from the right.

### 05 · hospital-5am — "The agency that fills the 5am shift gets the next req."
Five in the morning, still dark, heavy fog. A hospital tower rises like a monolith, its long grid of windows mostly unlit, the top floors dissolving into paper. A wide plaza and shallow steps lead to the entrance canopy. One tiny nurse in scrubs walks up the steps toward the doors, a bag over one shoulder, badge in hand. High on the tower, exactly one window is lit green, and a faint green glow spills from the entrance canopy onto the wet plaza. Nothing else is green. Camera low on the plaza, looking up, the figure small and alone in the frame.

### 06 · data-center — "Data centers and warehouses are where the volume is going."
Night. A data-center building sits on the plain like a black monolith: windowless, enormous, its edge lit by one line of hot cream where a floodlight rakes the wall. In front of it a loading yard with a row of docks, two tiny forklifts, a parked truck, and a tiny supervisor standing with a tablet. Drawn on the asphalt of the yard, as if projected, is a large geofence ring with tick marks around its circumference, glowing green; a single tiny worker is stepping across the ring toward the docks. Only the ring is green. Wide shot, camera high on a crane, fog on the horizon.

### 07 · coin-plaza — "Revenue and retention are two sides of one coin."
Dawn in a vast empty plaza between two monumental buildings. In the centre stands a coin the size of a house, on its edge, casting a long shadow across the paving. On the left face of the coin a relief of a worker in a hard hat; on the right face a relief of a customer's building and a rising chart, both rendered as carved stone with no lettering. On the left of the plaza a small crowd of tiny workers with duffel bags looks up at the coin; on the right a small crowd of tiny clients in office clothes with clipboards does the same. Only the coin's thin rim is green, catching the first light. Low camera, fog at the edges, grain heavy.

### 08 · before-after — "Same workers. Same sites. A different firm."
One wide interior of a staffing company's middle office, drawn twice in a single frame and split exactly down the centre by a thin vertical seam of bare paper, so the left half and the right half are the same room in two states. Left half, night: a dozen clerks hunched over desks buried in paper timesheets, filing cabinets spilling folders, three desk phones off the hook, sticky notes on every monitor, a wall clock reading eleven, a coffee pot on a hot plate, harsh overhead tubes. Right half, morning: the identical room with the same desks, now clear, two people standing and talking with coffee, sunlight through the same windows, the wall clock reading nine, and one thin green line of light running along the floor through the desks and out of the far door. Only that line is green. The camera is straight-on at desk height so both halves share one vanishing point; heavy grain on both, the left denser and darker.

## Station emblems — the suite of agents

Seven square emblems, one per station, used inside the "How it works" boxes and the station menu. They are objects that stand for the agent at that station: instruments and machines, not people. Each prompt is STYLE-B verbatim, a blank line, then the EMBLEM paragraph verbatim.

### STYLE-B (prefix for every emblem — do not vary)

A risograph print of a single object, square 1:1. Pure flat white background (#ffffff) with no grain, texture, gradient or vignette anywhere outside the object; the object floats centred with generous air around it and casts no shadow. The object itself is printed in two spot inks: deep charcoal-green (#0f1f17) for shadow and mass and HyperTrack green (#22c55e) for exactly one lit accent, with bare white paper as the highlight; heavy stochastic grain and halftone stipple inside the object only, slight misregistration of the two inks at its edges. The object reads like a monumental machine or scientific instrument in miniature, three-quarter view, dawn light from the upper left, serious and precise. No text, letters, numbers, logos, screens or UI. No cartoon, no clay, no photoreal, no glossy 3D render, no third colour.

### st-1 · ingest — Ingest
A brass intake hopper the size of a mailbox standing on four short legs: a wide funnel mouth on top into which a fanned stack of punched timecards is sliding, and a short conveyor tongue at its base from which a neat row of cards emerges. A single round indicator lamp on its face is lit green. Rivets, a small crank on the side, a hinged inspection door.

### st-2 · locate — Locate
A surveyor's beacon: a slender iron tripod carrying a lantern with a domed cap, planted on a small round ground plate. From the plate, three concentric geofence rings spread outward on the ground as thin arcs with tick marks around their circumference; the rings and the lantern's flame are green. A small compass rose engraved on the plate, no letters.

### st-3 · collect — Collect
A vintage desk telephone with a rotary dial and a heavy handset resting off the cradle, its coiled cord unspooling across the base and flattening out into a single paper timesheet with a ruled grid and no writing. A small clipboard leans against the phone's side. The one round lamp on the phone's base is lit green.

### st-4 · collate — Collate
A tabletop lightbox on short legs, seen from above and to the side, with four translucent sheets fanned across its surface at slight angles, each sheet carrying a different faint ruled pattern, and a thin steel ruler laid straight across all four. The lightbox glows green through the sheets; nothing else is green.

### st-5 · rules — Apply the rules
A cast-iron letterpress hand stamp with a wooden handle raised, poised over a single blank card on a small anvil block. The stamp face carries an abstract circular seal made of concentric lines and notches, no letters. The fresh impression already pressed into the card is green.

### st-6 · mediate — Mediate
A brass balance scale in perfect equilibrium on a stepped stone base, with a blank speech-bubble-shaped tablet resting on each pan, and a folded note with a wax seal tucked at the foot of the column. Only the wax seal is green.

### st-7 · pay — Pay
A sealed pay envelope standing upright, slightly fanned open at the top with the edge of a cheque visible inside, beside a short neat stack of coins, in front of a small brass teller's grille with an arched window. The envelope's wax seal is green.

## Overlay compositions (full-bleed sections with typography on the art)

Same STYLE block, same scenes, recomposed so one side is open for text. Each prompt is STYLE verbatim, a blank line, the base SCENE verbatim, a blank line, then the COMPOSITION clause verbatim.

COMPOSITION-RIGHT: Composition for text overlay: the entire subject and every figure sit inside the right 55% of the frame; the left 45% is open, quiet space (sky, fog, an empty plain or an empty floor) that dissolves smoothly into bare cream paper with no detail, no figures, no lines and no grain build-up, reserved for typography. The subject still reads at full monumental scale.
