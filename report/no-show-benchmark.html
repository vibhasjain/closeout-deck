The clock: the two industries split

The two industries move opposite ways with the clock. Healthcare's show rate improves on later shifts: 88% in the morning to 91% overnight. Light industrial's drops: 87% down to 85%, and drops further in several big markets (GA 87%→77%, PA 88%→79%, TX 86%→79%, OH 85%→79%).

The visibility behind the signals

How soon the signals arrive

Predicting a no-show takes one capability: the workforce becoming visible to the operation before the shift starts. From the moment a shift is assigned, telemetry helps improve visibility. On HyperTrack that happens early and almost universally, a case study ↗ showing its impact.

Show vs no-show: no-show visibility flattens at the leave-by

By the leave-by3, whoever is going to be heard from mostly has been: after it, visibility rises only +1.5% among no-shows, versus +3.5% among workers who show.

By the start, ~96% of workers who show have been heard from, but only 86% of healthcare no-shows and 72% of light industrial no-shows ever sent a signal at all. The gap is the earliest warning: a shift with no signal as the start approaches is already telling its story.

Most no-shows can be seen coming

The baseline no-show rate is 11.5%: 10.3% in healthcare, 13.2% in light industrial. Most no-shows announce themselves in the data first, and each one below is credited to the earliest signal on record: late departure (not moving by the time the commute requires), no location/message4 from the worker, worker switched off visibility, a long stop on the way, or a move away from the destination. Before any signal can fire, 30% of no-shows are cancelled before the expected departure3 (37% healthcare · 21% light industrial).

1 No-show = a scheduled shift where the worker never arrived. Figures reflect April–June 2026 across Healthcare and Light Industrial staffing (signals by state reflect May–June; trend figures are weekly), observed by HyperTrack telemetry.

2 Shift windows by start time, local to the site: Morning 5:00–11:59am · Afternoon 12:00–4:59pm · Evening 5:00–9:59pm · Overnight 10:00pm–4:59am.

3 Expected departure / leave-by — the leave-by = scheduled start − the normal drive time for that worker's home-to-site commute, set when the shift is scheduled, typically about 25 minutes before start. The expected departure = the leave-by plus a five-minute grace (a range, not an instant); a shift cancelled or closed inside it counts as cancelled before departure. Covers 95% of scheduled shifts (those with a commute estimate).

4 "No location/message" = not even a first tracking event has been received from the worker's device. "Heard from" / "visible" = at least one tracking event, a first location fix or message.
