* On-time = arrival within a five-minute grace of scheduled start, over workers who arrived. Dominant reason is assigned per late shift by minutes lost: departure minutes (how late they left vs their leave-by deadline) vs en-route minutes, credited to whichever is larger — for en-route, the specific commute reason (long stop / detour / traffic / vicinity / unexplained). When the worker was seen promptly but the first ETA was already past the start, the assignment — not the worker — is credited (last-minute assignment: the shift was typically assigned with less time left than the commute needs). Late shifts with no usable commute signal can't be verified and are excluded from the reason split. Only dimension values with at least 2,000 arrivals are shown.
