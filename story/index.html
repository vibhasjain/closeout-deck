HyperTrack 01 / 21

01 · Why we're here

We're here to protect your revenue.

Because you're about to get your lunch eaten by tech-enabled staffing firms. Especially in the age of AI.

02 · The gap

They will build and deploy AI faster than you.

That only accelerates the gap that is already widening between their growth and yours.

03 · Where AI landed first

You've already put AI to work at recruiting and matching.

Workers matched to jobs in real time, with high confidence. That was the first place AI landed.

04 · Where it lands next

The next place AI percolates to is the middle office.

Pay and bill. The better a firm gets at the middle office, the more reliable its worker pool becomes.

05 · Trust

When pay and bill work, workers trust the platform.

They know the payment is coming. They know it's going to be fast. They know it's going to be accurate.

06 · Churn

Pay them wrong and the door starts revolving.

Workers churn less when you pay them right. A rotating door of workers hits reliability directly, and compromised reliability is compromised revenue.

07 · The worker's marketplace

Every day, workers choose between you and two others.

That is the nature of flex work: everyone has flexibility. Every single day you have to make a good impression on the best, most reliable workers. Their marketplace is their phone.

08 · The cost of getting it wrong

Pay the wrong amount at the wrong time, and they pick another platform.

Worse yet, they leave you hanging at the last minute.

09 · The customer's marketplace

Every day, your customers are choosing too.

On the VMS they decide whether to pull workers from you or from the new kids on the block. Their marketplace is the VMS.

10 · The flywheel

If you don't pay workers as excellently as the new guys, the flywheel reverses.

Reliable workers win customers. Customers bring shifts. Shifts keep workers. Lag on pay, and it turns the other way.

11 · One coin

Revenue protection and worker retention are two sides of the same coin.

Neither is ever locked in. Both sides can choose again tomorrow. Which is why you cannot afford to lag, and why the middle office cannot be an afterthought.

12 · Now

Now you can notice exceptions and fix them in real time.

Location technology and SMS catch a shift going sideways while it is happening, engage the worker in the moment, and close out pay and bill accurately and fast.

13 · Payroll ops

Every shift has to answer two questions.

How much do I pay the worker? How much do I bill the customer? Every shift, every week, before the cutoff.

14 · Payroll ops

The answer comes from three sources that disagree.

The customer's clock or clipboard. The app the worker clocks in on. The phone's location. They arrive at different times and rarely say the same thing.

15 · Payroll ops

Onboard it like a digital worker.

It logs into UKG the way a person would, in a virtual browser, and pulls the punches out on a cadence. No integration project. Same for ADP, Ubeya, 7shifts, Paylocity and the rest.

16 · Payroll ops

The rulebook lives in people's heads.

State law, city ordinances, client contracts, facility quirks, this week's exceptions. Your best payroll person carries it all. When they leave, it leaves with them.

17 · Payroll ops

Compile the rulebook once. Run it on every shift.

Each rule is written in plain English, compiled once into a deterministic check, and applied to every shift before payroll runs. No prompting, no guessing.

18 · Payroll ops

Every flag traces to a clause. Every clause becomes a rule.

The agent raises the flag. The flag points at one paragraph in the wage order. That paragraph is compiled into one line the engine runs on every shift.

19 · Payroll ops

The agent asks the worker, not the clerk.

When a shift is missing a fact, the agent texts the person who has it while it is still fresh. The exception closes before anyone in the middle office sees it.

20 · Payroll ops

Humans handle only what needs a human.

Clean shifts go out on time. Flagged ones arrive with the rule, the evidence and the dollar delta already attached.

21 · The end state

Every decision shows its work.

Clock, location, clause, conversation, approval. Pinned to every payout and every invoice line. Pay right the first cycle, and the flywheel turns the right way.
